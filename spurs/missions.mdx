# Mission Domains

US-SPURS missions are organized around the 13 operating divisions, with Headquarters (Division 0 / 0000) providing strategic direction, enterprise governance, and cross-division coordination.

## Emergency Management

**Lead:** Division 1 / 1000 — Emergency Management

Core mission areas include:

- all-hazards preparedness
- disaster response and recovery
- emergency operations centers
- incident management and unified command
- crisis stabilization and continuity support
- mobile response and field coordination

## Infrastructure & Public Works

**Lead:** Division 2 / 2000 — Infrastructure & Public Works

Core mission areas include:

- roads, bridges, transit, ports, and airfields
- public buildings and critical facilities
- water, wastewater, drainage, and utilities
- construction, demolition, remediation, and reconstruction
- resilient urban and rural infrastructure
- capital-program engineering and public works

## Military Support

**Lead:** Division 3 / 3000 — Military Support

Core mission areas include:

- military support to civil operations
- readiness and mobilization
- military engineering and logistics
- ground, air, sea, and space support capabilities
- exercise and mission certification

## Law Enforcement & Investigations

**Lead:** Division 4 / 4000 — Law Enforcement & Investigations

Core mission areas include:

- criminal and administrative investigations
- protective services and facility security
- fraud, waste, abuse, and misconduct investigations
- evidence and case-management support
- public-safety partner coordination

## Health & Human Services

**Lead:** Division 5 / 5000 — Health & Human Services

Core mission areas include:

- mobile medical, dental, and vision services
- emergency and field healthcare
- public-health readiness
- healthcare access and voucher programs
- veteran, family, disability, and community support

## Education & Workforce Development

**Lead:** Division 6 / 6000 — Education & Workforce Development

Core mission areas include:

- United States Academy programs
- K–12 and adult education
- vocational and postsecondary education
- professional and technical training
- youth and adverse-youth programs
- tuition support and workforce development

## Housing & Homelessness

**Lead:** Division 7 / 7000 — Housing & Homelessness

Core mission areas include:

- homelessness outreach and assistance
- emergency and transitional housing
- shelter and sanitation support
- community stabilization
- residential and business relocation programs
- post-disaster housing recovery

## Science, Technology & ATLANTIS AI

**Lead:** Division 8 / 8000 — Science, Technology & ATLANTIS AI

Core mission areas include:

- ATLANTIS AI Multi-AI System
- SWARM as an integrated ATLANTIS module
- artificial intelligence and autonomous systems
- cybersecurity and secure digital infrastructure
- advanced computing and data systems
- research, development, prototyping, and technology transition

## Space, Aviation, Maritime & Mobility

**Lead:** Division 9 / 9000 — Space, Aviation, Maritime & Mobility

Core mission areas include:

- aviation and airfield operations
- maritime and shipyard programs
- space partnerships and launch-support systems
- unmanned aerial, surface, and subsurface platforms
- advanced transportation and mobility programs

## Energy & Environmental Resilience

**Lead:** Division 10 / 10000 — Energy & Environmental Resilience

Core mission areas include:

- microgrids and resilient power
- renewable and advanced-energy systems
- environmental cleanup and remediation
- climate adaptation and resilience
- endangered-species protection and relocation coordination

## Logistics & Procurement

**Lead:** Division 11 / 11000 — Logistics & Procurement

Core mission areas include:

- procurement and contracting support
- warehousing and inventory
- transportation, fleet, fuel, and equipment
- emergency supply chains
- field sustainment
- accountable-property management

## Finance & Enterprise Services

**Lead:** Division 12 / 12000 — Finance & Enterprise Services

Core mission areas include:

- budgeting and accounting
- financial controls and reimbursements
- human resources, payroll, and benefits
- records and administrative services
- enterprise facilities support
- internal policy administration

## Strategic Affairs & Global Operations

**Lead:** Division 13 / 13000 — Strategic Affairs & Global Operations

Core mission areas include:

- intergovernmental coordination
- regional and state-office alignment
- international and allied-partner engagement
- institutional partnerships
- strategic special projects
- global humanitarian and infrastructure-support missions

## Cross-Division Missions

Most major US-SPURS missions involve multiple divisions. A single lead division may own the mission while supporting divisions provide specialist capabilities.

### Example: Major Disaster

A major disaster may involve:

- Division 1 for incident command and emergency management
- Division 2 for infrastructure restoration
- Division 3 for military support
- Division 4 for public safety and investigations
- Division 5 for medical and human services
- Division 7 for housing and homelessness support
- Division 8 for ATLANTIS AI, data, communications, and technology support
- Division 10 for energy and environmental resilience
- Division 11 for logistics and procurement
- Division 13 for intergovernmental and external coordination

Headquarters provides strategic oversight and resolves cross-division priorities.

## Mission Lifecycle

US-SPURS missions generally follow a common lifecycle:

1. Situation assessment
2. Mission authorization and lead-division assignment
3. Joint planning and resource mobilization
4. Deployment and execution
5. Stabilization and recovery
6. Transition or demobilization
7. After-action review and corrective action

## Mission Priority Framework

Unless superseded by a controlling directive, the organizational priority order is:

1. Alameda County
2. California
3. United States
4. U.S. Territories and Allies
5. Earth
6. The Great Beyond

This framework establishes relative organizational priority; it does not prevent simultaneous missions across multiple regions.
