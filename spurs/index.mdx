# United States Department of Special Projects and Unified Response Services (US-SPURS)

US-SPURS is organized as an independent federal agency model with Headquarters as Division 0 / 0000 and 13 operating divisions spanning emergency response, infrastructure, defense support, public safety, health, education, housing, technology, mobility, energy, logistics, administration, and strategic affairs.

## Mission

US-SPURS exists to improve safety, stability, resilience, capability, and quality of life through coordinated response systems, public service, infrastructure development, advanced technology, and long-term operational support.

## Headquarters

### Division 0 / 0000 — Headquarters, US-SPURS

Headquarters establishes agency-wide priorities, mission authority, policy, doctrine, enterprise standards, governance, and cross-division coordination.

**Principal headquarters:**

1000 Patriot Way  
Joint Force Base Freedom Island, California 94500

## Operating Divisions

US-SPURS contains 13 operating divisions:

1. Unified Response and Emergency Management
2. Infrastructure, Engineering, and Public Works
3. Defense, Military Support, and Operational Readiness
4. Law Enforcement, Investigations, and Public Safety
5. Health, Medical, and Human Services
6. Education, Training, and Workforce Development
7. Housing, Homelessness, and Community Stabilization
8. Science, Research, Technology, and Artificial Intelligence
9. Space, Aviation, Maritime, and Advanced Mobility
10. Energy, Environment, and Climate Resilience
11. Logistics, Procurement, Supply Chain, and Fleet
12. Finance, Administration, and Enterprise Services
13. Strategic Affairs, Intergovernmental Coordination, and Global Operations

## Strategic Priorities

1. Stabilize communities quickly during crises.
2. Modernize critical infrastructure through resilient design and public works.
3. Maintain defense-support, public-safety, health, housing, and human-service readiness.
4. Build AI-enabled operational systems for planning, coordination, and execution.
5. Improve logistics, mobility, communications, energy, and supply-chain resilience.
6. Expand education, training, workforce development, and community capability.
7. Sustain long-term recovery and institutional improvement, not only immediate response.

## Geographic Command Structure

US-SPURS uses a layered structure consisting of:

- Headquarters
- regional offices
- state and territorial offices
- international offices, liaison elements, project teams, and temporary mission units where required

### Priority Order

5. Earth
6. The Great Beyond

## Operating Model

US-SPURS combines centralized strategic direction with distributed execution.

- Headquarters establishes priorities, mission authority, governance, and enterprise standards.
- Operating divisions maintain specialized capabilities and accountable leadership.
- Regional, state, territorial, and international elements coordinate geographic execution.
- Missions may use single-division command, joint task-force structures, or approved unified-command frameworks.
- ATLANTIS AI and supporting systems improve situational awareness, coordination, workflow execution, and forecasting.
