# ADR-0000: Decision Title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decision owners:** Names or accountable roles
- **Reviewers:** Affected technical, security, data, operational, and mission owners

## Context

Describe the problem, current state, constraints, and why a decision is required now.

## Decision

State the selected approach precisely enough that implementation teams can act without guessing.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1

Describe the option, strengths, weaknesses, and reason it was not selected.

### Alternative 2

Describe the option, strengths, weaknesses, and reason it was not selected.

## Consequences

### Positive

- List expected benefits.

### Negative

- List costs, constraints, and accepted risks.

### Neutral or Follow-On Effects

- List migration work, training needs, or future decisions created by this choice.

## Security and Privacy Impact

Document changes to trust boundaries, identity, authorization, data handling, secrets, auditability, and threat exposure.

## Operational Impact

Document reliability, observability, deployment, support, incident response, and degraded-mode implications.

## Migration Plan

Define implementation phases, compatibility requirements, data migration, and ownership.

## Rollback or Exit Strategy

Explain how the organization can reverse, replace, or retire the decision safely.

## Validation

Define the evidence that will show the decision is working as intended.
